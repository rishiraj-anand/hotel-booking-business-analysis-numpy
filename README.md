📖 Project Overview
The Hotel Booking Analytics Project is an end-to-end business analytics project developed using Python (NumPy). The project focuses on analyzing hotel booking data to evaluate booking performance, revenue generation, customer behavior, customer satisfaction, pricing strategies, and overall hotel business performance.
The objective of this project is to convert raw booking data into meaningful business insights through KPI analysis and provide strategic recommendations that support data-driven business decisions.

📑 Executive Summary
This project evaluates booking performance, revenue generation, customer satisfaction, pricing strategies, and operational efficiency using Python and NumPy. Through detailed business KPI analysis, the project identifies key revenue drivers, customer behavior patterns, and operational opportunities while providing strategic recommendations to improve hotel business performance and support informed business decision-making.

•	Reduce booking cancellations to minimize revenue loss.
•	Optimize promotional discount strategies.
•	Replicate best-performing hotel practices across lower-performing hotels.

•	Continue investing in premium room categories that generate higher revenue.
